About the Portal

A simple digital starting point for the college community.

Our purpose

Campus Portal is designed to help students access campus information in one organized and easy-to-use place. It brings together announcements, class schedule information, and learning resources.

Who can use it?

The portal is intended for students, instructors, and department staff who need a convenient way to find and share college-related information.

Accessibility feature

The site includes a keyboard-accessible “Skip to main content” link, visible keyboard focus indicators, readable color contrast, and descriptive alternative text for the logo.
